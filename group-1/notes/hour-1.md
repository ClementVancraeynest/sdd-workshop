# Hour 1 · The backlog

## Decisions
- Payment methods: card and cash. Reason: not given.
- VAT on screen: included in the price only, no separate line. Reason: not given.
- Returns without a receipt: refused. Reason: not given.
- Refunds: the cashier chooses card or cash for each return, whatever the original method. Reason: not given.
- Rounding: a discounted price is rounded down to the cent, in the customer's favour. Reason: not given.
- Returns: an article cannot come back more times than it was bought, over all returns of a sale. Reason: not given.
